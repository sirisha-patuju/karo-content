# Situation bank (pick a NEW one for every post)

Siri's rule (Nov 15): very different content every post, same idea underneath. The idea never changes: Karo takes care of your email and calendar, you just type (or say) what you need. What changes every time is WHO the person is, WHAT real moment they are stuck in, and WHICH job Karo does. Posts must feel like a real moment from a real day, with a time stamp or a specific stake, not a generic "too many emails" post.

## How to pick
1. Read logs/content-log.csv (columns persona and job). Never reuse a persona or a job from the last 12 posts. Prefer a persona and job not used yet at all.
2. Pick a situation below (or invent a new one in the same style: a specific person, a specific minute of the day, something at stake).
3. Pick a hook style that was not used in the last 3 posts.
4. Write the visuals and every line from that person's world: their senders, their subjects, their numbers. A realtor's inbox looks nothing like a teacher's.

## Hook styles (rotate)
Time stamp ("Tuesday, 6:12 PM. Three buyers want the same viewing slot.") | Confession ("I told a client I'd reply by Friday. It was Wednesday of next week.") | Costly mistake | Question ("What would you do with 6 hours back every week?") | POV | Quiet small win | Before and after | "Nobody tells you..." | A number story

## Karo jobs (one per post, rotate)
book a meeting | reschedule and tell everyone | fix a double booking | follow up on an email nobody answered | write the awkward email (late payment, saying no) | rewrite the tone | summarise a long thread or a weekend of email | clear promotions and surface the one email that matters | find an email or attachment | draft replies to many people at once | daily morning briefing | recurring weekly slot | handle a trip change (flight moved, meetings shifted) | reply in the sender's language (8 languages, only claim what the app shows)

## Carousel story arc (5 to 7 slides, vary the kinds)
1 the moment (time stamp, the problem visible: inbox, clash or notification flood) | 2 what is at stake | 3 the one sentence they typed (chat) | 4 what happened, shown as proof (calendar, sent mail or beforeafter) | 5 the takeaway line | last: cta. Change the order and kinds every time. Do not open with the same kind twice in a row.

## Single image
One striking moment from one persona: one visual plus one hook line. Different persona from the carousels the same day.

## Reels
Same bank: the flood and hook scene is that persona's world (their senders, their subjects, their numbers), the ask scenes show that persona's jobs, never the generic "563 unread" opening again.

## Closing idea (every caption, in different words)
Karo takes care of your email and calendar. The caption ends with the CTA: first 100 people, comment "Karo".
